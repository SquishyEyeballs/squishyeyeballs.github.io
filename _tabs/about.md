## Hi, I'm Mark

I work in IT with a background in infrastructure, networking and cloud support, and I'm building my skills towards a blue team and SOC analyst career. This blog is my public notebook: what I'm learning, how I work through problems, and what I'd do differently next time.

## What you'll find here

- **Malware analysis notes** on public samples, with defanged indicators and detection ideas
- **Reversing write-ups** using tools like Ghidra, objdump and tshark
- **Lab and learning notes** from Hack The Box Academy's Junior Cybersecurity Analyst path and practice challenges
- **Practical blue team angles**, such as what to look for in triage and which logs to check first

## How I work

I use a Windows machine with WSL and a Kali VM for lab work, and I keep detailed notes in Obsidian, which most of these posts start life as. I'm still early in my security journey, so expect honest write-ups that include the dead ends and corrections as well as the final answer.

## A note on scope

Everything here comes from public samples, lab environments and practice platforms. Nothing on this blog comes from an employer or client, and nothing here should be read as representing one.
